## Analytics

I use [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics) to see which pages get
read and how people find them. For each page view it may record the time, page URL,
referring site, approximate location (country, region, and city), browser, operating
system, and device type.

It does not use cookies and does not follow you across sites. Visits are counted using
a hash of the incoming request, which is valid for a single day and then reset. Recorded
page views are not tied to your IP address.
